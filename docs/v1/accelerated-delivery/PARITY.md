## Cross-owner guarantees and deliberate boundaries

- Eight D1 owners and private R2 stores sit behind nine Worker entrypoints, including the gateway. No owner writes another owner's database. There is no cross-D1 atomic transaction.
- Protected destination commits require durable prepared intent, current Core/Contacts/source evidence and stored owner pins. Source changes close new admission, request bounded cancellation, and remain pending until exact destination receipts resolve. Pin exemptions use both destination and operation ID. Expiry alone is not a release.
- Publication writes and verifies R2 before the D1 pointer transaction. Contacts, Roster, Venues, Layout and readable export delivery have independent outcomes. Receipt recovery retains the original operation and exposes the current publication pointer.
- Deploy publishes planning room references, not invented dated occupancy. Baseline `backend/DeployVenues.gs` supplies room/register references and publication windows. Verified Roster dated coverage supplies booking occupancy; absent coverage remains unknown. Used room references carry canonical venue revisions through publication fencing.
- Contacts content identity and read observation are distinct. Reads do not advance source revision/sequence. Published workload captures accepted facts and evaluation time; subsequent acceptance does not rewrite historical Wi. Its immutable cap basis, configured-cap flag, non-teaching reservation map and subject classifications survive publication/restart. Incomplete or missing reservations remain null; Roster computes the effective five-day cap from current baseline, confirmed Stapler, absences and Calendar parity. Older incomplete envelopes require explicit republication, not guessed zero reservations. New Roster workload decisions require a verified current publication and persist its identity.
- Provider jobs retain stable identities and distinguish accepted, delivered, failed and uncertain outcomes. Malformed/lost post-send responses are uncertain. A sender checks a current job deadline before each dispatch; delayed audience/authority checks are domain-specific. Unknown outcomes cannot be cleared by a generic retry.
- Restore verifies an isolated owner target, preserves committed history, fences restored prepared writers and treats dispatchable jobs from the older backup as uncertain. Suite reconciliation is required before activation; one owner's backup is not a consistent live snapshot of all owners.
