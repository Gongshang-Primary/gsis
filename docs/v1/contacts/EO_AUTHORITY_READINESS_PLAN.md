## Recommended representation and ownership

Keep the existing Contacts authority field **`eo`**, an explicit accepted yes/no record keyed by school and permanent Staff ID. It carries its own revision, effective interval and provenance. Role remains the job; EO Appt Level remains appointment information. Neither is a substitute for accepted EO evidence. No new parallel authoritative boolean is needed. Preserve the distinction between Education Officer employment status and the primary-school EO display tag: MKEs are Education Officers in MOE Kindergarten but do not receive that primary-school tag. The existing `schoolSection` classification and employment facts must retain this distinction; absence of the display tag must not manufacture Non-EO employment status. MKEs never hold SMC under the owner’s stated policy, so they cannot qualify through the SMC-and-EO Deploy route. Require accepted SMC=false evidence rather than inferring it from a tag at request time. Do not change independent Admin or School Leader policy through this clarification.

The owner accepted a Contacts screen showing **EO status, school section and appointment separately**. Retain the proposed appointment choices, with explicit “EO” (base/no named appointment) and “Non-EO” choices and approved named EO appointment choices. The owner’s MKE clarification means a universal two-choice presentation is insufficient: it must also preserve the MKE/MK classification without labelling those staff Non-EO or adding the primary-school EO tag. Show employment status, section and appointment distinctly within the Contacts editor, using existing classifications rather than another authority flag. This is a proposed v1 Contacts editing interface, not a change to the legacy derived Deploy column. An explicit accepted selection records the EO status and, where supplied, the separate appointment reference through the Contacts owner. A named appointment cannot silently create EO authority on read: it needs an accepted staff record and consistent status. Resolve conflicts during owner review; do not guess from ST, TC, a Role mapping, a teaching timetable, name suffix, or a blank cell.

A later write design must validate and commit related status/appointment changes atomically within Contacts' owning database, preserving earlier immutable revisions and stable operation identity. Deploy consumes the accepted projection and has no authority to backfill it. Do not reuse legacy Role-to-level recalculation to overwrite status. An owner-approved mapping may prepare suggestions for review; a suggestion or HR import is not accepted evidence. The verification source is the owner’s own review, not an HR list. Either Admins or Contacts Power Users confirm each record against that review, subject to the acceptance controls below. Both roles may perform all Contacts operations; catalogue definitions still use their own validated operation and revision contract, not implicit changes through a staff acceptance command.

Rejected alternatives: adding EO to Role would conflate jobs with employment classification and compete with ST/TC roles; deriving EO from workload eligibility misclassifies school leaders and incomplete profiles; blank-as-false fabricates evidence; changing only the picker leaves the legacy recalculation bug; automatic tag-based backfill bypasses acceptance. A separate EO yes/no control is a fallback presentation if the combined picker obscures status, using the same canonical `eo` field, not a second truth.

## Authority acceptance controls (PR128 M1, superseded role split by owner B1 decision)

The owner requested these additions after Claude's review at `74df90ae8c7bbb03234b4f2db8f7886dbb6d2ecd` (review published in PR129 at `8c056f22e331cd8520aa38172ced2fa502853abd`). The role split below incorporates the later owner decision recorded in PR134 review “Owner decision on B1” at afae51b4037fc14447a1d70a046b76321fd96be3. These controls constrain the future Contacts acceptance workflow; they are not implemented by this plan.

- **No self-acceptance:** nobody, including an Admin, may accept SMC, EO or School Leader records for their own permanent Staff ID. Resolve the acting identity to its trusted school/Staff ID; a different account or alias for the same person cannot bypass this check. Missing or ambiguous identity linkage blocks acceptance.
- **Either role alone:** an Admin or a Contacts Power User may propose and accept any Contacts record, including SMC and School Leader true or false, without approval from a second person or from an Admin. The proposer and accepter may be the same person, but the accepter must not be the subject of an SMC/EO/School Leader record. Current role, school, session and expected-revision checks still apply. This replaces the earlier Admin-approval requirement.
- **No notifications:** the owner explicitly declined Admin notifications on SMC or School Leader changes. Retain the full acceptance audit; do not add a notification job, recipient list or delivery gate.
- **Audit:** every acceptance records the authenticated proposer, authenticated accepter, acceptance time and stable operation ID, bound to school, subject Staff ID, field and accepted revision. Do not take actor identities from untrusted form fields. Preserve the acceptance and its audit evidence together within the owning Contacts transaction; an audit failure cannot produce a reported successful acceptance. Recover an uncertain result using the original operation ID before retrying.

This is a Contacts acceptance audit requirement. It must not be assumed to fit Core's existing security-audit projector, which strips extra fields. A later scoped write/audit contract must establish the durable record, access controls and recovery evidence. This requirement neither closes F5 nor adds a new retention policy. No self-acceptance exception or bootstrap bypass is authorised. If no eligible non-subject Admin or Contacts Power User is available, acceptance remains blocked. MKE+SMC must still be rejected. The owner accepted the residual risk of mutual SMC grants between Power Users; no extra approval or notification control is implied.

## Required completeness gate before T1D go-live

A read-only, authorised Contacts readiness report must enumerate **every active staff member in the school's Contacts directory**, including active provisional entries, with any missing SMC, EO or School Leader accepted record. Do not filter active staff to teachers or linked accounts. **Inactive staff are excluded from the blocking go-live population**; they may be listed separately for information, and their blank records do not block. No default or bulk values are written. Explicit false counts as complete. Pending/rejected proposals and blank values do not. Report missing evidence separately from expired, future-only, revoked, conflicting or malformed records; unresolved readiness errors for the active population block go-live. Unknown or unverified active status must not silently exclude a person: population coverage is incomplete until resolved. Preserve Core's existing interval evaluation semantics rather than changing runtime policy in this plan.

The restricted report contains school, permanent Staff ID, necessary display name, missing/invalid field names, reason, relevant source/field revisions and evaluation time. Keep staff rows out of public Git/CI logs and ordinary error responses. Verify identity uniqueness, directory coverage, catalogue/group dependencies and observation freshness. Run a single bounded consistent read inside the Contacts owner, returning the active population and its three authority records together with the population/authority epochs. A failed, truncated or inconsistent read is **incomplete**, never an empty success list. Recheck those epochs after evaluation and at cutover. Do not add page cursors or manifests unless measured size requires a separately scoped extension. A new active staff record, authority change or reactivation invalidates prior readiness evidence. Bind the report to the authoritative active-population revision as well as authority evidence. A reactivation during the scan prevents certification from that scan; a reactivation after it makes the prior result unusable until a fresh complete scan passes. Define the cutover recheck/fence in the later integration contract; there is no cross-D1 atomicity claim.

Go-live waits until the active population's missing list is empty, its other completeness/integrity checks pass, and Admin or Contacts Power User acceptance against the owner's review is evidenced. Inactive informational rows do not block and do not receive fabricated false records. A reactivated person without all three accepted authority records is denied with `AUTHORITY_EVIDENCE_MISSING` until the records are accepted under the controls above; reactivation alone grants no authority. Newly active provisional staff have the same completeness requirement. Re-run readiness for population/authority changes and maintain a bounded incomplete-record queue. This governs Contacts-derived staff access, preserving the independently authorised Core Admin route and existing read-only impersonation rules.

## Proposed non-retryable missing-evidence contract

Introduce `AUTHORITY_EVIDENCE_MISSING`: `outcome: 'deny'`, HTTP **403**, `retryable: false`. Use it only after a trusted, school/staff/query-bound current Contacts response establishes that a required authority record is absent or nonaccepted. Missing one or all three records follows the same honest classification; do not relax T1A's three-record requirement for SMC read in this change. User message: “Your authority records are incomplete. Contact a Contacts Power User.” Do not expose other people's fields or raw provider responses. Retrying unchanged data cannot repair it; an accepted correction permits a fresh evaluation.

Do not blanket-map PROVIDER_INVALID/503 to 403. Actual timeout/unavailable remains retryable unavailable; malformed/untrusted responses retain safe provider-error handling, stale evidence retains conflict semantics, explicit false remains complete data evaluated under the existing capability matrix. Accepted future/expired/revoked intervals are not silently relabelled missing. Independent Core Admin authority and read-only impersonation remain unchanged; the global readiness requirement is not a new per-request Admin dependency.

The isolated error-contract extension and adapter mapping are now merged in PR133 at 31886aca543305d4605261044a7cd8f13ea474d5; consumer integration remains separate. Core's audit projector validates known reason codes and discards unapproved fields; do not assume attaching missing-field lists or evidence objects persists them. Minimal approved reason metadata suffices for this denial. Durable observation recovery remains separate **F5 OPEN**; this proposal neither closes it nor changes source revision/sequence on every read.
