A referenced ID absent from Contacts remains an unresolved orphan; do not fabricate its authority, delete its assignment or reactivate it.

New/repointed IP, form-teacher and TG bindings and explicit Add existing actions require current accepted/effective activity=true, EO=true and applicable primary section.
