## Review focus

1. A UT duty exists in Deploy but not on the timetable, or is present in both; its capacity is neither lost nor counted twice.
2. A newer date/version request resolves before an older request; older results cannot replace its data or clear its loader.
3. An excluded period breaks a streak while remaining occupied and counted toward daily workload.
4. Logging is switched Off with an existing export pending, then On later; no off-interval text backlog or stuck prune/export state.
5. Admin email claims or sends fail, recipients change, or an issue resolves and reopens; retries preserve actionable state without duplicate successful messages.
