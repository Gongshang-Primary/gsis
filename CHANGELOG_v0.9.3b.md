# GSiS Roster v0.9.3b

Patch for GSiS v0.9.3 or v0.9.3a. Update Roster frontend, Roster Apps Script backend and the separate Roster notification Worker together. All other suite components remain v0.9.3.

- Save an authenticated absence report durably before allocation work. When the existing optional `GSIS_JOBS` D1 database is bound to both Workers, Pages records the report before calling Google. Without D1, the Apps Script Drive journal uses UserLock independently of the allocator's ScriptLock. Provider delivery and timetable preparation do not run in the capture path.
- Distinguish **received**, **applied to Roster**, **peer approval pending**, **peer approved** and **needs review**. Preserve the exact report, actor, request ID and trusted receipt timestamp across retries, closed browsers and midnight. A received receipt confirms durable capture; it does not claim allocation or peer approval.
- Drain received reports independently of the automatic-allocation and WhatsApp sending settings during ordinary cron. The existing diagnostic-only mode intentionally remains read-only; keep `WHATSAPP_DIAGNOSTICS_ENABLED=false` or unset for production processing. Apply the core absence before separately validating instructions and peer proposals. A stale instruction or failed peer proposal cannot erase the illness report. Keep unresolved work visible to the reporter and SMC; permit audited SMC withdrawal of an unforwarded D1 report awaiting review, with an explicit reason.
- Hold affected-date allocation, publication and timetable notifications when a known core absence is still pending. A new D1 receipt arriving during a commit is processed by the next update; D1 and Apps Script do not form one distributed transaction.
- Fix repeated false source clashes: generated timetable-package `preparedAt` values no longer change the semantic source proof. Real timetable, staffing and source changes still require revalidation.
- Treat proposed relief/combine arrangements as tentative reservations for both the affected lessons and the proposed colleague's periods. The allocator respects these reservations. Explicit peer acceptance checks current conditions and becomes protected human relief. Rejection, expiry and an audited SMC override release the reservation; stale approval links cannot restore superseded cover.
- Send independent WhatsApp and email invitations. Email uses the current CES (`schools.gov.sg`) and GSPS (`gsps.edu.sg`) addresses in Contacts and opens the authenticated My Relief Duties page. Delivery errors do not roll back absence capture; an email with an uncertain provider outcome is retained for review and is not blindly resent.
- Show the receiving colleague and pending/approved peer status in Assign Relief. Keep Own arrangements choices in the form until the final submission.
- Remove **Absent full day w/o relief instructions**, as requested. Preserve explicit **Periods and timings**, **Select All**, **Clear periods**, **I'm not available during the above selected periods**, Relief Instructions, Own arrangements and **Submit absence**.

No new secret or WhatsApp template is introduced. D1 forwarding requires the existing Roster `BRIDGE_SECRET` to be present on the notification Worker as well as Pages and Apps Script; verify that binding during upgrade. `ROSTER_ABSENCE_INTAKE_FILE` is created and maintained automatically. Source modules remain separate for maintenance and are bundled into one complete Apps Script upload. No separate active product backlog is carried by this patch.

Reports rejected by the backend as invalid or unauthorized remain available for review but do not hold allocation. A business conflict still requires review; no rejected report is represented as an applied absence.
