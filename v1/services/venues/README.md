# Venues owner

`domain.mjs` preserves the existing pure Venues booking and policy rules from the attributed production source. `service.mjs` persists those rules through indexed D1 records, interval conflicts, per-record revisions, per-venue occupancy epochs, durable receipts and notification jobs. `projection.mjs` validates large source publications in bounded immutable chunks.

Bookings, amendments, cancellations, approvals, recurring occurrences, transfers, waivers, closures, linked spaces, buffers, room policies and grants remain owner operations. A waiver can waive source occupancy only; it cannot bypass another booking, closure, pending source publication or current authority. Disjoint rooms can commit concurrently.

Before Deploy or Roster commits a source publication, Venues verifies its original Core decision and exact prepared source operation. A durable scope fence prevents a booking from slipping between source checking and source commit. Deploy planning-reference room IDs/revisions have metadata-only fences; they do not fabricate timed occupancy. Roster supplies real dated coverage.

Large projections carry a content-addressed manifest and up to 2,000 chunks of at most 256 intervals. Each source-begin invocation validates up to four chunks while a coarse venue/date fence remains held. Existing reservations are checked in the owner database. Source commit requires a sealed manifest; staged rows become visible only when Venues adopts the source's immutable committed receipt. Restart resumes the cursor. An unknown or expired source outcome retains the fence. A terminal abort must prove a later source fence before release. No cross-D1 atomicity is claimed.

Reads use bounded 31-day occupancy windows; booking commands revalidate their actual requested dates. Confirmed daily Roster heads supersede baseline rows for that date. Current-source verification and final SQL guards prevent cached availability from authorising a stale booking. The full-school synthetic D1 fixture includes 40 rooms × 17 periods × 60 dates (40,800 intervals).

Notifications store an outcome per staff member and channel, check current owner participants and purpose-limited Contacts evidence, and verify the active job fence before every send. Accepted recipients are not resent; unknown outcomes survive restart. WhatsApp requires the existing approved six-field template. Verified WhatsApp callbacks enter through a named receipt binding. Immutable callback evidence can precede the sender receipt and is reconciled after restart; read/delivered status cannot be downgraded by an older callback. A generation guard and completed recipient plan prevent a partial or interrupted send from being labelled fully delivered. WhatsApp proof does not claim email delivery. Callbacks never enqueue a send. Existing provider tokens remain deployment inputs and are not rotated by this code.

Focused verification: `tests/v1-product-venues.mjs`, `tests/v1-product-venues-staged.mjs`, and `tests/v1-product-venues-notifications.mjs`, and `tests/v1-product-venues-receipts.mjs`, all with `TZ=Asia/Singapore`. These use actual local Miniflare D1 with synthetic Core/source/provider boundaries. Live integration and runtime UAT remain separate gates.
