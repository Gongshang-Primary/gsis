# GSiS v0.7.17

Baseline: v0.7.16 at `60f7a92fe8b2e4604c8136ff000ae531e9c52ff6`. The user authorised the next release and Stapler troubleshooting on 19 October 2026, Singapore time. This release addresses queued items 018 through 021.

## Saved Stapler plans

The compressed-plan reader now reconstructs the gzip Blob with its content type and filename before decompression. The stored bytes, `gzip-base64-v1` envelope and schema remain unchanged. A strict local adapter reproduces the old failure and accepts the corrected Blob. That verifies the metadata correction under the stricter host contract; it does not establish the cause of the school's production failure.

Opening an existing saved plan avoids the quota reads and complete baseline-cell reconstruction that its editor does not need. The accepted saved plan supplies the editing day. Current source, Calendar and authority checks remain in force. Save and Confirm continue to perform the full operational checks.

Load errors retain the saved record and expose the available diagnostic reference, backend version and failure stage. The required installed check opens the existing 21 October Revision 1 draft and compares its final tiles and blank periods. Deleting, resetting or importing over that record is not part of this release's recovery procedure.

## WhatsApp Inbox

Inbox reads avoid loading the separate operations store when duty-alert eligibility is not needed. Successful mutations return the authorised conversation snapshot, removing the immediate second thread request. Current authority, revision checks, exact retries and durable reply queueing remain in force.

Conversation refresh updates message content without rebuilding unchanged messages. The interface preserves reply drafts and playing audio during refresh. A queued reply remains queued until the separate notification Worker dispatches it. This release makes no production latency or delivery guarantee.

## Calculations

The own-record calculation read removes a redundant Deploy index check, reducing index reads from three to two. Published-source validity, identity, revision and recorded-history checks remain in force. The weekly formula and actual figures are unchanged.

The five-step explanation describes the school teaching-demand average, the teacher's role allowance and the saved SIUE preparation and marking allowance. It uses each teacher's actual values. It does not present the saved SIUE percentage as an independently verified national average.

The explanation also states that an appointment holder's offload is already included in the weekly quota. KP status alone does not provide an additional preference to be assigned last. Review found no separate KP-only deferral to remove from the existing allocation paths. Other eligibility and saved business rules retain their effect.

## Home navigation

The Home tile title is **Stapler**. Its description is **Suspended Timeable Advance PLannER**, preserving the requested spelling and capitalisation. The planner heading, route, icon, role filtering and menu position are unchanged.

## Deployment and verification

An existing v0.7.16 installation needs the complete Deploy backend published as a new web app version and the Roster frontend. The full package includes other components with current version labels. The existing v0.7.16 notification Worker remains compatible, so this targeted update does not require its redeployment.

See VALIDATION.md, PERFORMANCE_v0.7.17.md and RELEASE_SUMMARY.json for the checks actually completed. Installed Drive readback, school-service latency, outgoing reply delivery and private voice playback require separate operational verification.
